M4-files.txt -- which Z32/ files the M4 paper needs.

Paper: "A machine-checked confinement atlas for {xi(3/2)^n}" (plan-cert32.html
section 11.1, milestone M4, lane (b); Math. Comp. primary, Exp. Math. fallback;
18-25 pp + tables).  Nine sections; the section numbers below are the paper's,
per the table in section 11.1 -- NOT the plan's own section numbering.

  1 framing ([BD05] contrast)        6 unions and the largeness curve
  2 carry coding + KILL/CYCLE        7 the band (cycle enumeration)
  3 the soundness theorem            8 verification + the R-5 bug exhibit
  4 the length-1/3 line              9 limits and disclosure
  5 past the 1995 line

Three marks:
  [R]  required -- the paper cites it, prints a number it produced, or must
       build to make a cited Lean name check
  [A]  artifact-only -- not cited in the prose, but inside M4' item P3's
       "gencert.py, the three engines, reproduce.sh, checksummed data/"
  [-]  not needed for this paper

================================================================================
B.  ENGINES AND GENERATORS
================================================================================

[R] gencert.py              sections 3, 5, 6, 8.  Emits every funnel in
                            BlockCert.lean (reproduce.sh diffs them byte for
                            byte); --closed --ranked is M4' item P1; refuses the
                            five in-print-nonempty sets in both modes.  Named
                            explicitly in P3.

[R] atlas.c                 sections 5, 6, 7, 8.  Engine C, the load-bearing
                            one: the exact y-model for arbitrary unions.
                            Produces the frontier L*=1466/3600, every union
                            record and control, the horse cycle enumeration of
                            section 7, and the falsification test of section 8.

[R] hold.c                  sections 4, 5, 8.  Engine A (theta-model): the X1
                            escape orbits and the length-1/3 sweep to G=3888,
                            and the first X2 pass.

[R] gridcert.c              section 8 only.  Engine B -- it prints nothing in
                            the paper (P4 excludes its grid lambda column, and
                            section 9 calls those values artifacts), but it is
                            one of the four implementations whose agreement
                            section 8 reports, so it must ship and build.

[R] verify_atlas.py         section 8.  The independent Python/Fraction
                            re-implementation, and the cross-check that caught
                            the R-5 union-merge bug that section 8 exhibits.

[R] x3climb.py              section 6.  Provenance of the 120- and 240-cell
                            unions, hence of the 0.7417 record (Tier 1
                            headline).  The climb itself is quarantined from
                            every theorem statement; the union it found is
                            verified three independent ways.

[R] prodcert.py             sections 7, 9.  Not a paper section of its own (M7
                            is separate and gates nothing), but section 7's
                            claim as scoped -- "band entries carry exponentially
                            many confined orbits, so no hold-set-only argument
                            can settle them" -- is FALSE at the frontier, where
                            the count is 1.  The corrected scoping and its
                            evidence come from here, so this ships as the
                            scope-guard on section 7 whether or not Theorems A/B
                            are told in the paper.

[A] pqcontrols.py           M6, a second base.  No paper section; reproduce.sh
                            runs it and checksums its output, so it belongs in
                            the artifact.  It is also what licenses stating
                            section 3's soundness theorem in the (p,q) form.

[-] probe_pq.py             M6/M7 feasibility probe, superseded by pqcontrols.py
                            and prodcert.py; not in the README file table, not
                            run by reproduce.sh, no number of its own.
